## `billboard_impact.csv`

Source copy:
[`billboard_impact.csv`](https://github.com/matheusfacure/python-causality-handbook/blob/2edbd69f2e3629dd2f6ec805c72f0dbe651d713a/causal-inference-for-the-brave-and-true/data/billboard_impact.csv)

This is synthetic data generated by the upstream
[`Simulated-Data.ipynb`](https://github.com/matheusfacure/python-causality-handbook/blob/2edbd69f2e3629dd2f6ec805c72f0dbe651d713a/Simulated-Data.ipynb)
with NumPy seed `123`. It was introduced by Matheus Facure in upstream commit
[`0c3898c`](https://github.com/matheusfacure/python-causality-handbook/commit/0c3898c377241cdeaae210e422c801923c326dd0).

The generator draws four groups from gamma distributions followed by Bernoulli
zeroing:

| `poa` | `jul` | Meaning in the example | Rows |
| ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| 1 | 0 | Porto Alegre, pre-period (May) | 500 |
| 1 | 1 | Porto Alegre, post-period (July) | 800 |
| 0 | 0 | Florianópolis, pre-period (May) | 1,300 |
| 0 | 1 | Florianópolis, post-period (July) | 2,000 |

Fields:

- `deposits`: simulated integer outcome;
- `poa`: simulated treated-city indicator; and
- `jul`: simulated post-period indicator.

The handbook's chapter 13 explains that the real motivating experiment is
confidential and that this published scenario is only similar to it. Do not
present these rows as observations from real customers, cities, or a real
marketing campaign.

## `smoking.csv`

Source copy:
[`smoking.csv`](https://github.com/matheusfacure/python-causality-handbook/blob/2edbd69f2e3629dd2f6ec805c72f0dbe651d713a/causal-inference-for-the-brave-and-true/data/smoking.csv)

The file was introduced by Matheus Facure in upstream commit
[`1d2615b`](https://github.com/matheusfacure/python-causality-handbook/commit/1d2615b82b5f68dd79904180e80aa47352a2d978).
It contains a balanced panel of 39 U.S. states over 31 years (1970–2000) used
for the California Proposition 99 synthetic-control example.

Fields:

- `state`: numeric state identifier used by the source dataset;
- `year`: calendar year;
- `cigsale`: annual per-capita cigarette sales, in packs;
- `lnincome`: logged per-capita state personal income;
- `beer`: per-capita beer consumption;
- `age15to24`: share of the state population aged 15–24;
- `retprice`: average retail price per cigarette pack, in cents;
- `california`: handbook-added indicator for the treated state; and
- `after_treatment`: handbook-added indicator for years after the 1988 passage
  of Proposition 99.

Scientific reference:

> Alberto Abadie, Alexis Diamond, and Jens Hainmueller (2010), “Synthetic
> Control Methods for Comparative Case Studies: Estimating the Effect of
> California's Tobacco Control Program,” *Journal of the American Statistical
> Association* 105(490), 493–505.
> <https://doi.org/10.1198/jasa.2009.ap08746>

The article's Appendix A gives the following source chain:

- cigarette sales and average retail prices: Orzechowski and Walker (2005),
  *The Tax Burden on Tobacco*, historical compilation, volume 40;
- state personal income: U.S. Census, *United States Statistical Abstract*,
  converted to 1997 dollars using the Consumer Price Index;
- population and the percentage aged 15–24: U.S. Census Bureau; and
- per-capita beer consumption: Beer Institute's *Brewer's Almanac*.
